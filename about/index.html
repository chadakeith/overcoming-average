About

Chad Keith. Davidson / Charlotte.

I'm Chad Keith. Overcoming Average started as a podcast out of Davidson / Charlotte - long conversations with people who built something, rebuilt after a hard season, or just decided average wasn't the plan.

Episodes live here and on the usual podcast apps. Guests talk business, faith, family, tech, and the messy middle of figuring life out. No scripted hype. Just real stories and the odd rabbit hole.
